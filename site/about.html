About us

Pour & Co. started as a cart with one grinder and a kettle. Today it is a small bar with the same idea: make each cup well.

We buy green coffee directly from farms and roast it in small batches every week, so what you drink is never more than a few weeks old.

We keep the menu short on purpose. It lets us taste every brew before it goes to you.
